Six ways to
reveal an image

Each section uses a genuinely different mechanism — not one wipe at six angles. They fire once on scroll; hit Replay on any of them to run it again.

Scroll
01

Aperture

A circular clip opens from the centre while the photograph itself counter-zooms from 1.45× down to 1×. The opposing motion is what sells the depth — the frame grows outward as the subject settles back.

02

Ink Bleed

A soft radial mask grows outward while an SVG turbulence filter distorts the edge and settles to zero. The boundary crawls and feathers like ink soaking into paper rather than a clean geometric edge.

03

Split-Flap

Eight horizontal panels hinged along their top edge, each holding its own band of the image. They fall flat one after another like a departure board settling — edge-on to face-on, with real perspective.

04

Letterbox

Opens from a hairline slit at the centre line, expanding vertically to full height. The image inside is squashed to 1.7× on Y and un-squashes as the frame grows, so the picture appears to stretch into its own shape.

05

Scanline Dissolve

A repeating gradient mask of 10px bands, whose opaque portion widens from zero to full. The picture materialises through a fine comb — no extra DOM, one element, purely the mask doing the work.

06

Hinge

The whole frame is a door hinged on its left edge, swinging from 88° edge-on to flat. A gradient shade rides on top and lifts as it turns, so the surface catches light the way a real panel would.
